About us

From tannery floor
to your workbench.

Hide started as a cutting table in the back of a shoe workshop, sourcing offcuts the big suppliers wouldn't sell in small runs. We now work directly with a handful of independent tanneries, buying full hides and splitting them to order rather than holding warehouses of pre-cut stock.

That keeps the range small on purpose — finishes we know well, in quantities that make sense for one maker or a studio of twenty — rather than a catalogue too wide for anyone to actually get to know.

Get the leather in your hands
before you commit.

Tell us what you're making and we'll put together a swatch set from the range. Most go out within 48 hours.
